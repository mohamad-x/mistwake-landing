Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This page explains what information MistWake collects through mistwake.online and the $1 VIP reservation flow, and how that information is used. MistWake is a pre-launch project currently preparing for a Kickstarter campaign.

What we collect

  • Email address — if you join the free launch list or complete a $1 VIP reservation, we collect the email you provide so we can send you launch and campaign updates.
  • Payment information — if you complete a $1 VIP reservation, payment is processed directly by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details; Stripe's own privacy policy governs that data.
  • Basic usage and analytics data — we use Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, and Google Analytics to understand which pages and ads lead to reservations and sign-ups. These tools may set cookies and collect device/browser information in line with their own privacy policies.

How we use this information

We use your email to send updates about the MistWake Kickstarter campaign, including launch notifications and access to your VIP pricing. We use aggregated analytics data to improve this page and our advertising. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from email updates at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us at support@mistwake.online. You can request a refund of your $1 VIP reservation within 30 days by emailing the same address.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@mistwake.online.

This is a general-purpose policy describing our current data practices and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you operate under specific regulatory obligations (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), have this reviewed by a lawyer before relying on it.
