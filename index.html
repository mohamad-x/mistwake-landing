Mist, not mess

No soaked pillow. No water chaos.

MistWake is designed around a light, controlled mist cue — not a heavy splash. The nozzle aims the cue toward your wake-up position.

Safety and trust

Designed with water separation in mind.

The water reservoir, pump, and electronics are separated inside the housing. The nozzle directs mist toward the bed — not across your nightstand.

Easy cleanup

Hidden side drawer for cleaner mornings.

The drainage drawer is designed to catch excess water out of sight so the nightstand setup stays simple and easy to maintain.
