Why I built this

Sound alone was not enough.

I've missed early meetings, flights, and things that mattered — not because I didn't set an alarm, but because I slept right through it. MistWake is the alarm I needed: sound first, then a controlled physical cue when sound is not enough.

The problem

Heavy sleepers can learn to ignore sound-only alarms.

Most alarms rely on one channel: noise. If you are a heavy sleeper, your brain can tune it out, silence it automatically, or sleep through it completely.

MistWake keeps the familiar sound alarm, then adds a second cue: a light, controlled mist aimed toward your wake-up position. The goal is not to scare you awake. The goal is to make the alarm harder to dismiss while you are half asleep.

Why MistWake?

A second sensory trigger for the moment sound fails.

Sunrise clocks use light. Smart speakers use sound. Phone alarms use sound and vibration. MistWake adds a different physical cue: a controlled mist trigger designed specifically for people who sleep through normal alarms.

That makes the product instantly understandable, visually memorable, and Kickstarter-friendly: it solves a familiar problem in a way people have not seen before.

Safety and trust

Designed with water separation in mind.

MistWake keeps the water reservoir, pump, and electronics physically separated inside the housing. The nozzle directs a controlled mist toward the bed — not across your nightstand or electronics.
