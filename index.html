How It Works

Simple. Annoying enough to work.

Set the alarm, fill the tank, choose your wake-up mode, and MistWake activates sound plus a controlled spray when it is time to get up.

1. Set your alarm

Choose your wake time and mode.

2. Fill the tank

Add water before bed.

3. Wake up

Sound and spray make the alarm harder to ignore.
